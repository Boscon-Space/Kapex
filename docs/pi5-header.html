Raspberry Pi 5 — 40-pin header, physical numbering

The board has no printed numbers. Count from the end of the header nearest the microSD slot. Pin 1 is the pin with the square solder pad (look at the underside of the board); every other pad is round. Odd pins are the inner row (toward the middle of the board), even pins are the outer row (toward the board edge).

Loopback jumper for gpio_probe: one wire between pin 11 (GPIO17) and pin 13 (GPIO27). Both are on the inner row, next to each other: the 6th and 7th pins counting from the microSD end.
Bench BMP280 on I²C: VCC → pin 1 (3.3 V), SDA → pin 3 (GPIO2), SCL → pin 5 (GPIO3), GND → pin 9. Pins 1, 3, 5 are the first three inner-row pins; pin 9 is the 5th inner-row pin. Never put the sensor on 5 V.

BMP280 wiring (bench sensor, GY-BMP280 3.3 V module)

Four wires. Read the module's pin labels printed on its board; the usual order is SDO, CSB, SDA, SCL, GND, VCC. Leave SDO and CSB unconnected, exactly as they were on the Heltec rig: the module then sits at I²C address 0x76 (or 0x77 — the probe scans both). The module is 3.3 V only: VCC goes to pin 1, never to a 5 V pin.
